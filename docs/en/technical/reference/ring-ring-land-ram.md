# Ring Ring Land RAM

[English documentation](../../README.md) | [日本語](../../../jp/technical/reference/ring-ring-land-ram.md) | [CARDA overlay](../architecture/carda.md#sending-and-returning-pets) | [Save file format](save-file.md)

Ring Ring Land is the PocketStation game Legend of Mana sends your pets to. The
port rewrites it for the Dreamcast's VMU, and this page is the map of every RAM
byte it uses, in both places it lives: the PocketStation's work RAM, where the
original keeps it, and the 1 KB `vmu_ram` the VMU-shaped rewrite keeps it in.

The source of truth is `pocketstation/src/game/ram_map.txt`; the table below is
generated from it by `pocketstation/tools/gen_ram.py doc`, and a test keeps the
two the same.

## Reading the table

- **VMU** is the field's offset in `vmu_ram`. `pocketstation/tools/pack_ram.py`
  chose it from how often the seven test scripts touch each byte: after the
  runtime's own block at `0x000`, fields that are not arrays come first, busiest first, since only they gain from the VMU
  CPU's directly addressed first bank, then the arrays, then a small stack
  reserve. A `-` means the VMU has no use for the field.
- **Hardware** is where the field lives on the VMU itself: an offset below
  `0x100` is RAM bank 1 at the same address, and the rest is WRAM at the offset
  less `0x100`. The stack reserve has none, as the VMU's stack lives in bank 0.
  The [VMU runtime](vmu-runtime.md) page describes the runtime's own blocks.
- **PocketStation** is the original address, in work RAM from `0x200`. Bytes no
  script reads are left out.
- **Type** is the original's; a second type is the VMU's where they differ.
- **Meaning** describes the original. Four-byte hex numbers such as `0200234C`
  are the PocketStation functions that use a field.

Some fields must stay side by side because the original reads them together:
`idle_lo` and `repeat_up` make one 16-bit counter, `mode_wait` and `wait_hi`
another, the four scratch bytes from `scratch_wait` and `scratch_lo`/`scratch_hi`
are read as halfwords, the three reel strips are indexed from `strip_a`, the
128-byte state block from `points` to `save_tail` is saved to the card as it
stands. Their VMU offsets keep those orders. The reel window draws past the
last row of `fb` into `blit_a` and `blit_b`; the VMU draws straight into its own
frame buffer instead of keeping `fb`, and sends those rows to the two blit
buffers by name.

On the VMU itself, offsets below `0x100` are RAM bank 1, the application's
directly addressed bank, and the rest are WRAM from address `0x000`;
`gen_ram.py hw` writes both as assembler equates, and no field crosses from one
to the other. The stack reserve is left out there, as the VMU's stack lives in
bank 0. The first 32 bytes of bank 1, `rt_reserved`, belong to the VMU runtime:
its indirect registers R0-R3 sit at `0x00`-`0x03`, and no game field goes there.
Bank 1's last seven bytes, `rt_input` at `0x0F9`-`0x0FF`, hold the runtime's
input state, and the packer keeps game fields out of them too.
WRAM's last 24 bytes, `rt_save` at `0x2E8`-`0x2FF`, hold the pet save's block
list the runtime finds at reset, so the stack reserve sits past WRAM's end.

Three fields change form on the VMU. The interrupt slots in `irq_handler` and
`fiq_driver` held code addresses and hold small handler ids instead, and
`snd_start` held a card address and holds an offset into the game's file. The
two kernel pointers, `kernel_calls` and `com_flags`, have no VMU counterpart. The
VMU times a sound's end instead of playing its samples, so `snd_start`,
`snd_pos` and `snd_len` mean less there, and the Phase 1 comparison skips them.
